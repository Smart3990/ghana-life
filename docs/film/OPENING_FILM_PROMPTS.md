# Ghanaverse opening film: video prompts

Four clips, one per picture in `apps/web/public/film/`. In the video tool, choose **image-to-video**, upload the clip's picture as the **first frame**, and paste **the style block + the clip's prompt** together. Settings for every clip: 16:9, 1080p (720p for the phone build), 24 fps, 8 to 10 seconds, realistic motion, no music burned in (music and voices are added in the game so they can be muted and subtitled).

All people are invented. No logos, brand names, readable signs, weapons or real public figures.

---

## Style block (paste at the start of every clip)

Photorealistic cinematic film, shot on 35mm film with an anamorphic lens, natural film grain, gentle halation around bright lights. Warm West African light: golden hour, soft haze, dust in the air catching the sun. Colours are rich but natural: red earth, faded kente reds, oranges and yellows, deep brown skin tones rendered with real texture and warmth. Movement is slow, smooth and steady, like a camera on a dolly or a gimbal; no shaking, no sudden zooms, no cuts inside the clip. People move naturally, with real weight and breathing, blinking and small hand movements; faces stay consistent from the first frame to the last. The mood is hopeful, emotional and quietly epic, like the opening of a feature film about leaving home. Keep the first frame exactly as the uploaded picture and build the motion from it. No text, no subtitles, no logos, no watermarks.

## Clip 1: Dawn in the village (picture: `village.webp`), 10 seconds

**Scene:** A small Ghanaian village at dawn. On the left, an elderly grandmother in a red, orange and yellow kente-pattern wrap and matching headscarf sits on a carved dark wooden stool in front of her cement-block house with a rusty corrugated zinc roof. A red earth path runs between low block walls into the mist. Two tall coconut palms stand against a pink-orange sky. A cockerel stands on the low wall on the right.

**Camera:** Starts on the exact first frame, then a very slow dolly push-in towards the grandmother, drifting slightly to the right, ending as a medium shot of her face and shoulders with the path and palms soft behind her.

**Action, second by second:**
- 0 to 2 s: Stillness. Mist drifts slowly left to right along the path. Palm fronds sway gently. The sky brightens a little.
- 2 to 4 s: The cockerel lifts its head, puffs its chest and crows; its tail feathers shift in the breeze.
- 4 to 7 s: The grandmother breathes slowly, hands resting on her knees. She hears footsteps, turns her head towards the path and her face softens.
- 7 to 10 s: A gentle, proud smile spreads across her face; her eyes shine a little, as if trying not to cry. The first sunlight touches her cheek and the edge of her headscarf.

**Lighting:** Low sun behind the palms, warm rim light on her headscarf and shoulder, cool blue shadows in the doorway, mist glowing gold.

**Sound (for reference; added in the game):** cockerel crow, distant birds, a goat bleating far away, a broom sweeping a yard somewhere, soft wind in the palms.

**End frame:** A warm close shot of the grandmother smiling towards the path. Hold still for the last half second so it can fade into the next clip.

**Avoid:** changing her clothes or face, extra people appearing, modern cars, text on walls, the cockerel flying away, fast camera moves.

## Clip 2: Grandma's ₵50 (picture: `hands.webp`), 8 seconds

**Scene:** An extreme close-up at dawn. On the right, the wrinkled hands of the grandmother, her kente-pattern sleeve in red, yellow and black, hold a few worn, folded Ghana cedi notes. On the left, a young person's open hand reaches in from the edge of the frame. Behind them, soft out-of-focus thatched and zinc roofs, palm trees and golden mist.

**Camera:** A very slow push-in with a slight downward tilt, keeping both hands sharp; the background stays creamy and out of focus. Ends a little closer on the notes and the two palms.

**Action, second by second:**
- 0 to 2 s: The grandmother's fingers carefully smooth and fold the notes once more, slowly, with love; her knuckles move naturally.
- 2 to 5 s: She places the folded notes into the young palm and closes the young fingers over them with both of her hands, holding them there.
- 5 to 7 s: Her thumb gently pats the back of the young hand twice: a blessing. The young hand trembles slightly, then grips the money.
- 7 to 8 s: Her hands stay wrapped around the young hand. Stillness. A speck of dust floats through the sunbeam.

**Lighting:** Sun low and behind, backlighting the edges of the fingers and the paper; warm skin tones, deep shadows in the palms.

**Sound (for reference):** paper rustling, a soft breath, distant morning birds. Grandma's line is added in the game as a subtitle and voice: "Go and make us proud."

**End frame:** Both hands joined around the folded notes, centred, very still.

**Avoid:** extra fingers or deformed hands, notes changing into other objects, readable currency text, jewellery appearing, faces entering the frame.

## Clip 3: The bus mate (picture: `bus.webp`), 9 seconds

**Scene:** A dusty red-earth roadside in rural Ghana in the early morning haze. On the left, an old white minibus (a trotro) with an open sliding door; a roof rack piled with bags, a sack and a wooden crate. A young bus mate in a faded brown T-shirt leans out of the doorway, one hand gripping the roof, the other arm waving high, mouth open, shouting. On the right, women walk past carrying wide trays of fruit and goods on their heads; passengers with bags walk towards the bus. Palm trees and golden mist behind.

**Camera:** Starts on the first frame, then a slow lateral tracking move to the left along the side of the bus, with a slight push-in, as if the viewer is a passenger walking to the door. Ends with the bus mate in a medium shot, the open doorway inviting.

**Action, second by second:**
- 0 to 3 s: The bus mate waves his arm in big circles, shouting with energy; his body bounces with each call. Dust lifts from the road around passing feet.
- 3 to 5 s: A woman with a tray on her head walks steadily through the frame on the right, perfectly balanced, her wrap swinging. A man lifts a bag up to the roof rack.
- 5 to 7 s: The bus mate looks straight towards the camera, points at it with a big grin and beckons with his hand: "you, come".
- 7 to 9 s: He slaps the side of the bus twice and steps back into the doorway, making room. The engine idles; the bus rocks slightly. Exhaust haze drifts in the low sun.

**Lighting:** Hazy morning sun from the right, backlit dust, warm highlights on the white bus, soft shadows under the trays.

**Sound (for reference):** the bus mate shouting "Accra! Accra! Accra! Last seat, come, come!", the engine idling, a horn tapped twice, chatter, sandals on dirt. The voice is added in the game.

**End frame:** The open doorway of the bus with the bus mate holding the door, the seat inside waiting.

**Avoid:** readable writing or slogans on the bus, brand badges, the bus driving away, people walking through each other, trays falling, faces melting in the crowd.

## Clip 4: Arriving at Circle (picture: `circle.webp`), 10 seconds

**Scene:** A huge, busy lorry station in central Accra in the late afternoon, seen from a slightly raised position. In the foreground, seen from behind and slightly to the side, a young newcomer with short hair, a dark jacket and a small grey backpack over one shoulder stands still, taking it all in. Ahead, rows of white minibuses and taxis with yellow-painted corners, crowds of people, women carrying trays and basins on their heads, colourful umbrella stalls, hawkers. A long concrete road flyover crosses the background, the low sun glowing behind it in golden haze.

**Camera:** A slow crane-up and push-forward over the newcomer's shoulder, rising gently so more of the station and the flyover come into view, as if the city opens up in front of them. Ends as a wide, majestic shot of the station and the flyover in the sun, with the newcomer small in the lower left corner.

**Action, second by second:**
- 0 to 3 s: The newcomer takes a deep breath; their shoulders rise and fall. They tighten their grip on the backpack strap.
- 2 to 6 s: The station is alive: minibuses edge forward and back; a taxi creeps through the crowd; women with head trays weave between vehicles; a hawker lifts a bag of pure water sachets; umbrellas flutter; dust and exhaust glow in the sunbeams.
- 6 to 8 s: The newcomer slowly turns their head from left to right, taking in the whole city; we see a hint of their profile: amazed, a little nervous.
- 8 to 10 s: Traffic moves steadily on the flyover in the background, and a flock of birds crosses the sun. The camera settles on the wide view and holds.

**Lighting:** Golden-hour sun low behind the flyover, strong backlight and lens flare, long shadows towards the camera, warm haze over everything.

**Sound (for reference):** the roar of the station: horns, engines, bus mates calling destinations, sellers calling "pure water!", music from a kiosk speaker. Added in the game.

**End frame:** A wide golden view of the station and the flyover, held still, ready to cut to the game's 3D camera coming down to the street.

**Avoid:** readable signs, logos or brand names, real landmarks changed into other cities, vehicles merging into each other, people walking through vehicles, sudden camera shake.
