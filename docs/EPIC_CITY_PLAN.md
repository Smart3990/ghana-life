# Epic City plan (Ghanaverse)

The owner bought the Low Poly Epic City pack (Polyperfect) and wants everything in the game to use it,
on the real Accra map. One step at a time; each step goes to the test site first, has a "done when"
test, and goes live only after the owner has seen it work. Agreed with the owner on 27 Oct 2026.

## Rules that hold in every step

- **Crowded everywhere:** both pavements of every road full of people; every road full of cars, with
  jams at junctions. The crowd never disappears on a slow phone: far-away people and buildings get
  simpler instead.
- **Nobody sells on the road:** kiosks, stalls, tables and sellers stand only on pavements and open
  ground, never on the tarmac or the kerb.
- **Real Accra:** real roads, roundabouts, flyovers and places; the pack supplies the look.
- **Smooth on the cheap Android** (Light tier inside its budget).

## Steps

1. **Done: pack models into game files.** All 438 models converted; the catalogue page `/catalogue`.
2. **Done: roads.** The pack's tarmac, kerbs, pavements, lines, junctions, roundabout islands,
   flyovers and zebras on every real Accra road.
3. **Day and night, and street lights.**
   - The sun follows the real Accra time and date: morning, midday, golden afternoon, sunset about
     6 pm, night.
   - Street lights all round the city: the pack's road lamps in pairs facing each other across every
     road (close together), arms over the road; pavement lamps between them lighting the walkways.
     Yellow light at night. The pack's tall traffic lights at the junctions, easy to see. (Done.)
   - At night every street light, window, sign, billboard and headlight is lit; real light pools on
     the road near you.
4. **Vehicles and traffic.**
   - The pack's vehicles replace ours; trotros from the pack's van and okadas from its motorbike, in
     Accra colours.
   - The pack's traffic rules: follow the car ahead, stop at red lights, give way, go round
     roundabouts, climb the flyovers.
   - Crowded roads everywhere; the main roads jammed in the evening rush hour. (Done; the pack's
     traffic rules: following, red lights and waiting are ours, kept.)
5. **Done: people, the story characters included.** (Faces on everyone close; the still crowd from Quaternius's Posed Background Characters, the walkers and story people from the Quaternius bodies, the Epic City pack's people on every vehicle you can see into; about twice the crowd at the same cost; nobody inside kiosks or vehicles. The owner's phone check of the frame rate is still needed.)
   - The whole crowd from the pack's people, with Ghanaian skin tones and Accra clothes colours.
   - The player, Auntie Dede, Uncle Kwame, the thief and everyone in the story rebuilt from pack
     people.
   - People never walk into kiosks, stalls, cars or each other; crowded pavements everywhere.
6. **Buildings and street things.**
   - Pack buildings on the real Accra building shapes. (Done, 6b: places, flats, offices, houses, villas in the wealthy areas.)
   - Kiosks, stalls, umbrellas, benches, bins, signs and trees from the pack. (Done, 6d: stands, umbrellas, food carts, bus shelters, benches, bins, trees and palms; our container kiosks stay.)
   - **Circle junction packed with sellers:** rows of kiosks and stalls so full that you can see at once
     this is a busy market place, all on the pavements and the open ground, none on the road. (Done, 6d.)
   - Uncle's compound in Nima rebuilt in the pack's style. (Done, 6c: a quiet street of pack houses off a main road; his white modern family house, his wife and children; your room at the back.)
7. **Done: the living world.** (Trains on the railway with level crossings, planes at Kotoka, a helicopter, a hot-air balloon and boats at the coast, 30-31 Oct 2026.) Trains on the real railway with level crossings; planes taking off and
   landing at Kotoka; a helicopter, a hot-air balloon and boats at the coast.
8. **The story scenes, redone in the new world.**
   - Auntie Dede's stall in a better place: on a busy pavement near Circle, beside the zebra, in view,
     not on an island and not near the road's edge.
   - All the film shots done again for the new look: the trotro arrival, the snatch seen from behind,
     the chase, Auntie's stall, eating, Uncle's car, the ride home, the compound and the room.
9. **Final check on the cheap Android.** Speed (fps), loading, crowd and traffic, the whole story
   played through; fix whatever is slow.

Later: rain, wind and storms.
